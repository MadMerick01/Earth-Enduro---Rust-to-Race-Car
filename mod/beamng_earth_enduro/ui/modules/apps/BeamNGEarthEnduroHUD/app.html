Tips
  • Copy the coordinates on the Route tab, paste them into the map search and teleport there to start or resume.
  • You earn $250 for every kilometre you drive. Teleports earn nothing.
  • Your sponsor pays $4,000 for every 10 km of driving: the bar under your money shows how far to the next one. Teleports do not count.
  • Reaching a stage's finish pays a $25,000 stage complete reward. A CarKov extraction pays $15,000.
  • Save often. Money, mileage and parts since your last Save are lost when you exit or load the level again.
  • One save: you live with your damage and your bills. There is no wreck limit, but a car you cannot drive and cannot pay to fix leaves you stuck: restart the stage, or quit the run on CarKov.
  • Stages are optional: start any of them from the Stages tab. Your car and money come with you, and a restart takes you back to how you started the stage. A ✓ marks the stages you have finished.
  • Keep some money back for repairs. Repairs cost more on pricier cars: a wreck can cost up to three quarters of what the car is worth.
  • Parts taken off are traded in for 30% of their value. Trying parts on and swapping back is free.
  • Driving more than 25 m with an unpaid parts or car bill puts your paid-for car back.
  • Beat the clock: the time limit counts down while you drive and stops while the game is paused. Every repair, and every new car, costs you 5 minutes. Buying parts costs no time.
  • Pace compares your time with how far you are, in a straight line, from start to finish. Winding roads can put you behind early on and ahead later.
  • Missing the time limit is not game over: the clock keeps counting in red as overtime.
  • CarKov Off-Road, on the CarKov tab, is a side run you can enter at any time: pay to be dropped somewhere random on Earth and reach the extraction point for a big payout, then go back to your stage.
  • Roadside events, on stages and CarKov runs: every 20 km there is about a 1 in 3 chance of one. A friendly village may offer a free, better part for your car; Install fits it and repairs your car for free, with no time penalty, and you keep your perk. Or bandits may make off with $100 to $1,000 of your money.
